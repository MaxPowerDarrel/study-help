# 2026 Hope Bible Reading Plan

This is the year that we're going to read through the *whole Bible* together. Are you ready?!

It sounds intimidating, but you can do it! Check out the schedule: less than 20 minutes per day, 5 days per week, with catch-up days built in.

Every morning, you'll get an email from me with a 60-second devotional video based on our reading for that day. Feel free to respond to that email with any questions or thoughts about what we read! The first time I read through the Bible from beginning, I jotted down every single question I had. I still have them today, and it's fun to read through them. Most of them have been answered, but some answers have to wait until heaven.

I'm excited to do this *together*! My goal is to keep this exercise devotional for me too, so these videos will be pretty informal, probably taken from my back porch, with just a few thoughts from the text. But, I'm always game to dive in deeper with you if you have questions!

God *will* bless your time in his Word. He promises it! (Check out Isaiah 55:10–11.) So, let's dive in!! — Pastor Nate
